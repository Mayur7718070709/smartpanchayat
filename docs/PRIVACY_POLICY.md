This draft must be reviewed by Indian privacy counsel and the participating Panchayat before publication. It describes the current production design; enabled providers and retention periods must be confirmed before approval.

## Information we process

We process account identifiers and contact details such as name, mobile number and email; citizen profile details such as address, ward, date of birth, gender and profile photograph; service applications, uploaded documents and application history; complaints, feedback and assistant conversations; notices and read status; and dues, payment order, transaction, refund and receipt metadata. We do not intend to receive or store card, UPI PIN, CVV or banking credentials; the payment provider handles payment credentials.

Technical records may include authentication/session identifiers, request IDs, timestamps, device and application diagnostics, security events and network information needed to protect and operate the service. The app does not request precise device location in the current release.

## Why we use information

Information is used to verify identity and eligibility; provide Panchayat services; process and track applications, complaints and payments; deliver documents, notices and support; prevent abuse and fraud; maintain audit records; comply with law; and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information or use it for behavioural advertising.

## Service providers and disclosures

Information is processed only as necessary by authorized Panchayat personnel and contracted infrastructure/service providers, currently including Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Render for the API service and operational logs; the configured SMS provider for OTP delivery; and Razorpay when payments are enabled. Legal, regulatory or law-enforcement disclosure may occur when required. Provider names and enabled features must be rechecked at each release.

## Tenant separation and security

Citizen and Panchayat information is logically separated by tenant and user identity. Access controls, private storage, encryption in transit, least-privileged database access, audit logging and rate limits are used. No system is risk-free; suspected incidents are investigated under the incident-response procedure.

## Retention

Records are retained only for the period required to deliver services, satisfy Panchayat record schedules, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and comply with law. Assistant conversations can be deleted through supported controls. Exact retention periods for applications, certificates, complaints, payments, logs and backups must be approved and published before launch. Backup copies expire according to the approved backup schedule.

## Choices and rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion where legally permitted, withdraw optional consent, and raise a grievance using the published privacy contact. Some statutory Panchayat and financial records cannot be deleted immediately. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request. Account deletion must also be available through a public web request route before store submission if it is not available in-app.

## Children

The service is intended for eligible residents using Panchayat services. The lawful basis and guardian process for minors must be approved before accepting accounts for children.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the effective policy, and notices or renewed consent will be provided when required.

## मराठी सारांश

Smart Panchayat नागरिकाचे नाव, मोबाईल क्रमांक, ईमेल, पत्ता, वॉर्ड, जन्मतारीख, लिंग, प्रोफाइल फोटो, सेवा अर्ज व कागदपत्रे, तक्रारी, अभिप्राय, सहाय्यक संभाषणे आणि देयक/पावतीची माहिती पंचायत सेवा देण्यासाठी प्रक्रिया करते. कार्ड क्रमांक, CVV, UPI PIN किंवा बँकिंग पासवर्ड साठवण्याचा आमचा उद्देश नाही; पेमेंटची संवेदनशील माहिती अधिकृत पेमेंट प्रदाता हाताळतो.

माहितीची विक्री किंवा वर्तनाधारित जाहिरातींसाठी वापर केला जात नाही. अधिकृत ग्रामपंचायत कर्मचारी आणि आवश्यक सेवा प्रदाते—Supabase, Render, OTP SMS प्रदाता आणि पेमेंट सक्षम असल्यास Razorpay—मर्यादित उद्देशासाठी माहिती प्रक्रिया करू शकतात. नागरिकांना कायद्यानुसार माहिती पाहणे, दुरुस्ती, हटविणे किंवा तक्रार नोंदविण्याचा अधिकार असू शकतो. अंतिम संपर्क, डेटा राखण्याचा कालावधी आणि कायदेशीर मंजुरी प्रकाशनापूर्वी जोडणे आवश्यक आहे.
